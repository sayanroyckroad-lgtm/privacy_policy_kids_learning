🔒 Family & Children Safety First Kids Learning Easy is designed specifically for children and families. We strictly do NOT collect, transmit, share, or sell any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) of children or parents. All learning progress is kept 100% offline on your device.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Kids Learning Easy ("we", "our", or "us"). We provide an interactive, bilingual educational mobile application designed for preschool and primary school children to learn the Bengali alphabet, English alphabet, counting, basic arithmetic, and vocabulary through gamified quests, stickers, and sound feedback.

This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles user information in strict accordance with the Google Play Developer Program Policies (including the Families Policy), the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), and global data privacy standards (such as GDPR-K).

2. Target Audience & Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Our application is designed for children under the age of 13, including age groups:

  • Starter (Ages 3–5): Early letter identification, shapes, and phonetic pronunciation.
  • Explorer (Ages 5–7): Word matching, basic addition, subtraction, and vocabulary.
  • Wizard (Ages 7+): Multiplication, division, and spelling challenges.

In strict compliance with COPPA and Google Play’s Families Policy:

  • We do not require user registration, account creation, or login.
  • We do not ask for children’s names, emails, physical addresses, phone numbers, or photographs.
  • We do not condition a child's participation in any educational activity on the disclosure of personal information.
  • We do not enable social chat, user-to-user messaging, or public profile publishing.

4. Device Permissions & Audio Usage

The application requests only minimal, necessary system permissions to operate its educational features:

  • Audio Output / Text-to-Speech (TTS): Used exclusively on-device to pronounce letters, numbers, words, and celebration cheers. No audio or voice recordings are made, captured, or transmitted.
  • Vibration / Haptic Feedback: Used optionally to provide gentle tactile feedback when answering quizzes correctly or unlocking a trophy.
  • Storage (Internal Sandbox): Used strictly within the app’s secure local sandbox to save stickers and reward trophies.

5. Advertising & Google AdMob Compliance (Families Policy)

To support ongoing development and keep this educational experience free for all children and families, the app may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob (a Google Play Families Certified Ads SDK).

In strict adherence to the Google Play Families Policy, COPPA, and GDPR-K:

  • Child-Directed Treatment Tagging (COPPA): All ad requests sent to Google AdMob are explicitly tagged with tagForChildDirectedTreatment(true) and tagForUnderAgeOfConsent(true).
  • Non-Personalized Ads Only: We strictly serve non-personalized, contextual advertisements. No tracking cookies, behavioral targeting, cross-app profiling, or advertising identifiers (such as AAID) are used to target children.
  • Family-Safe Content Rating: Maximum ad content rating is restricted strictly to G (General Audiences) suitable for preschool and young children. Ads containing mature themes, violence, dating, gambling, or age-inappropriate content are blocked by design.
  • No Deceptive or Disruptive Ad Formats: Ads do not interfere with normal gameplay, do not mimic interactive educational buttons, and follow Google Play's interstitial/rewarded guidelines.

For more information regarding Google's child-directed advertising standards, please review the Google Privacy Policy and Google AdMob Child-Directed Treatment Documentation.

6. Data Security and Retention

Because all gameplay scores, badges, and learning history are stored locally on the user's Android device:

  • Data remains strictly on your device and under your parental control.
  • Parents and users can completely reset or delete all game progress at any time by clearing the app data via Android Settings > Apps > Kids Learning > Storage > Clear Storage / Clear Data, or by uninstalling the application.

7. Parental Rights and Inquiries

Parents and legal guardians have full authority over their children's data. If you have any questions regarding our privacy practices, wish to review what data is handled, or have suggestions to make the app even safer for children, you may contact us directly.
